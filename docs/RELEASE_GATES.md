# Reader-facing publication gates

This repository is a **public first-edition sampler**. Research and technical limitations are documented in [PUBLICATION_STATUS.md](PUBLICATION_STATUS.md). Attribution and redistribution limits appear in [RIGHTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md](RIGHTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md).

## What was accepted for this edition
- Seven self-contained exhibits (the mathematical ones retain their independent toolchain requirements).
- Full owner-supplied Paper B R0007R3 reader and both reviewed oral histories, with its original source records and source-checksums intact.
- Complete flagship Jacobi manuscript and selected Lean project; precise producer/checker and padding statement.
- Generalized Raccoon/Oxbow architectural overview **instead of private operations documents**.
- A deliberately synthetic, retired-public Catch exercise **instead of private benchmark keys**.
- File-level digest manifest and a local verifier.

## What remains open and is disclosed, not silently upgraded
- No common build of all included Lean projects; no new claims of external mathematical refereeing.
- Exact K1 unpublished predecessor title/identifier and a K1-specific independent proof replay.
- Jacobi's general finite-array extension/padding contract and any formal deployment of its checker in an authorization runtime.
- Full fidelity of The Catch to its original private scoring design.
- Original field-guide chapter-by-chapter selection, future chess research and runtime security guarantees.
- Source-specific third-party licensing and additional reuse permissions. See the rights matrix.

Publication does not change the mathematical claim grades or convert speculative components into completed infrastructure. If a claim appears too strong, open an issue with the exact file and statement.
