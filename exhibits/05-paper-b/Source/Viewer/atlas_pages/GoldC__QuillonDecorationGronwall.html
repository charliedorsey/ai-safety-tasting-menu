298/-! ## Sign-safe exact decomposition (replaces any "sandwich" of a signed sum)
299
300If a coupled mask is written exactly as `G = A + E` with `E ≥ 0`, then for any signed
301summand `F` with `|F| ≤ C` and nonnegative weights `w`, the error made by replacing `G`
302by `A` is at most `C · Σ w E`. No upper/lower sandwich of a signed sum is used. -/

306    |∑ i ∈ S, w i * F i * G i - ∑ i ∈ S, w i * F i * A i| ≤ C * ∑ i ∈ S, w i * E i := by
307  rw [← Finset.sum_sub_distrib, Finset.mul_sum]

309  rw [hdec i hi, show w i * F i * (A i + E i) - w i * F i * A i = F i * (w i * E i) by ring,
310    abs_mul, abs_of_nonneg (mul_nonneg (hw i hi) (hE i hi))]
311  exact mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_right (hF i hi) (mul_nonneg (hw i hi) (hE i hi))
