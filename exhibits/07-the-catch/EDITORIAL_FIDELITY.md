# The Catch — fidelity review of our public adaptation

**Finding:** The current synthetic five-case prototype is technically safe, but it does not yet capture the difficulty or distinctive flavor of the original 19-case real-work experiment.

The original supplied archive includes 19 cases across mathematics, engineering, review, refusal and relational contexts, with clean decoys, erroneous reasoning, partial truths, explicit surviving facts and evidence probes. It distinguishes `define`, `number`, and `source` probes, REFERENT versus ECHO, pre-read/locked judgments, optional revision and scoring for false convictions, reason quality, signal detection, mercy and probe economy.

The current prototype reduces that to simpler arithmetic/Python/cryptography examples, one predetermined probe, optional partial-survivor notes and basic classification counts. This is a **substantive intellectual downgrade**, not merely anonymization.

**Remaster plan:** Preserve the private 19-case archive outside the public repo. Create five or more **new, challenging cases inspired by the original types of epistemic failure** rather than copying its private text. Restore a meaningful choice of three probe types with a small shared budget, require explicit surviving-truth identification on partial answers, and score false alarms and probe economy independently. Preserve a clean-but-surprising mathematical item. Do not invent automated semantic grading of open-text reasons. Retire every exposed case from future blind model evaluation.

The existing five-case HTML remains a functional technical starter, **not the final product**. Run a small cold-reader comprehension test after a substantive remaster.
