# Tasting order

## Five minutes

Try [The Catch](../exhibits/07-the-catch/index.html), then see [how its current prototype differs from the richer original](../exhibits/07-the-catch/EDITORIAL_FIDELITY.md). Ask whether each new statement is an independently checkable referent or just an echo.

## Fifteen minutes

1. **Start with the question.** Read [the top-level thesis](../README.md).
2. **See a proof in context.** Open [KMS's offline reader](../exhibits/02-kms/OPEN_ME.html), then its [proof guide](../exhibits/02-kms/site/proof_guide.html) and source atlas.
3. **Touch a certificate.** Open the [Jacobi executable producer/checker tour](../exhibits/03-jacobi/PRODUCER_CHECKER_TOUR.md) and see why finding a witness and checking one need not be the same operation.
4. **Try judgment.** Play [The Catch](../exhibits/07-the-catch/index.html). Beware persuasive echoes, including from yourself.
5. **See the architecture.** Scan the [generalized field guide](../exhibits/04-tool-field-guide/README.md) and the [runtime vision](RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md).

## An afternoon

1. Inspect [WEIL](../exhibits/01-weil/README.md) and reproduce one source-bound proof, including its scoped hypotheses.
2. Compare the informal KMS paper with its formal theorem atlas.
3. Read the complete Jacobi manuscript alongside its producer, checker and finite-array padding caveat.
4. Explore the **complete** [Paper B R0007R3 original reading room](../exhibits/05-paper-b/OPEN_ME_READING_ROOM_HERE.html), including K1 and the two oral histories.
5. Try the full [SHIU](../exhibits/06-shiu/README.md) with its own pinned toolchain.

## A question to take away

**Which assurances are directly enforced before an AI-produced artifact or request is allowed to do something consequential?**
