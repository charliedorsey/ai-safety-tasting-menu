Where each mathematical move comes from

Sixty-two mechanism-level scholarly cross-references. These distinguish a published technique, its exact formal-source implementation, the location in the canonical paper, and participant credit. The historical atlas and mathematical paper remain unchanged.

How to read these citations. A classical bibliography entry credits a method or gives context; it does not prove Paper B's clipped interval, source mask, signed coefficient or exponent. Elementary algebra may have no single original paper to cite. For the source's “Suzuki” label, no distinct published Suzuki origin was verified: the proven reciprocal-sum application is traced to Karatsuba/Vinogradov-style methods and its actual Lean source, without claiming a separate Suzuki publication. The individual project contribution of a named Lean module's eponym is likewise unverified.
