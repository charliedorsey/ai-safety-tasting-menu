5# The prime-power theorem with the exact gcd loss
6
7`kgcd N a b = gcd(a, b, N)` (computed from the canonical representatives).
8
9* `kSum_eq_kloostermanUnitSum` — `kSum` is the toolkit's `kloostermanUnitSum` (`rfl`).
10* `norm_kSum_prime_le` — `k = 1`, all coefficients: from the toolkit's Weil bound `prime_bound`
11  (and the trivial bound when `a = b = 0`).
12* `norm_kSum_unit_primePow_le` — unit coefficients, `k ≥ 2`, every prime `p` (including `2`):
13  `‖S(a,b;p^k)‖ ≤ (k+1) √(p^k)`.
14* **`norm_kSum_primePow_le`** — every `k ≥ 1`, all `a, b`:
15  `‖S(a,b;p^k)‖ ≤ τ(p^k) · √(gcd(a,b,p^k) · p^k)` with `τ(p^k) = k + 1`,
16  by induction on `k`: units (cancellation) / exactly one coefficient divisible by `p` (vanishing) /
17  both divisible (`S(pa',pb';p^k) = p·S(a',b';p^(k-1))`, the gcd absorbing the factor `p`).
18-/
