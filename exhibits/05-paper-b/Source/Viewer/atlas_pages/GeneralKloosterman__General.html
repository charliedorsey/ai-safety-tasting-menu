4# General modulus: CRT assembly and the Weil–Estermann bound
5
6* `kgcd_crt` — the gcd loss is multiplicative across the toolkit's Bézout/CRT splitting
7  (the local coefficients are unit multiples of the reductions).
8* **`norm_kSum_le`** — for every modulus `N ≥ 1` and all `a, b ∈ ZMod N`:
9
10    `‖S(a,b;N)‖ ≤ τ(N) · √(gcd(a,b,N) · N)`,
11
12  `τ(N) = #N.divisors`. Proof: induction over coprime prime-power factorizations
13  (`Nat.recOnPosPrimePosCoprime`), using the local theorem `norm_kSum_primePow_le` at each prime

16* `norm_kSum_le_of_isUnit_left/right` — a unit coefficient: `‖S‖ ≤ τ(N) √N`.

93    exact this

111/-- **General-modulus Weil–Estermann bound.** For every `N ≥ 1` and all `a, b ∈ ZMod N`,
112`‖S(a,b;N)‖ ≤ τ(N) · √(gcd(a,b,N) · N)`. -/
113theorem norm_kSum_le (N : ℕ) [NeZero N] (a b : ZMod N) :
114    ‖kSum N a b‖ ≤ (#N.divisors : ℝ) * Real.sqrt (kgcd N a b * N) :=
