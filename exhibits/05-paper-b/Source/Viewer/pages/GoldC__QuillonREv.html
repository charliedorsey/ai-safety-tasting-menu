    5# Quillon CSC3 F8.11: parameters and eventual bounds for the R cell (fixed `r`)
    6
    7**Parameters**, with `E = e^{Λ^{1/4}}` and `ε = Λ^{−1/200}`:

   16* `R_combine`: the final real combination.

   18No axiom, no `sorry`.
